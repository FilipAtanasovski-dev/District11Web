Tradition & Passion

Our Story

GOOD FOOD, GREAT COMPANY, MEMORABLE MOMENTS

District 11 Brunch Bar & Restaurant was created with a simple idea: bring together delicious food, quality drinks, and a welcoming atmosphere under one roof. What started as a passion for good food and hospitality has grown into a place where friends, families, and guests can come together and enjoy every moment.

Our menu combines familiar favorites with carefully prepared dishes made from fresh, quality ingredients. From relaxed breakfasts and brunches to satisfying lunches, dinners, desserts, and refreshing drinks, we aim to offer something for everyone. Every dish is prepared with care and served with the attention our guests deserve.

For us, a restaurant is more than just a place to eat. It is a place to celebrate, relax, meet friends, and create memories. We are proud to welcome our guests every day and look forward to making every visit special with great food, friendly service, and a warm atmosphere.

Join Our Team

WOULD YOU LIKE TO BE PART OF OUR TEAM?

Responsibilities
  • Welcome guests and provide friendly, attentive service throughout their visit.
  • Present menus, explain dishes, and answer questions about food and drinks.
  • Take food and beverage orders accurately and efficiently.
  • Serve meals and drinks while maintaining a high standard of presentation.
  • Keep tables, dining areas, and service stations clean and organized.
  • Work closely with the kitchen and bar to ensure smooth service.
  • Handle guest requests and help create a welcoming restaurant atmosphere.
Qualifications
  • Previous hospitality experience is an advantage.
  • Friendly, professional, and positive attitude.
  • Strong communication and customer service skills.
  • Ability to work well in a team environment.
  • Ability to work flexible shifts, including weekends.
Responsibilities
  • Prepare and serve alcoholic and non-alcoholic beverages to guests.
  • Provide friendly and professional service at the bar.
  • Prepare cocktails, coffee, soft drinks, and other beverages according to house standards.
  • Maintain a clean, organized, and well-stocked bar area.
  • Take orders and process payments accurately.
  • Assist guests with drink recommendations and menu questions.
  • Work closely with the restaurant team to ensure efficient service.
Qualifications
  • Previous bartending or hospitality experience is an advantage.
  • Good knowledge of beverages and basic cocktail preparation.
  • Friendly and confident communication style.
  • Ability to work efficiently during busy service periods.
  • Responsible, reliable, and comfortable working as part of a team.
Responsibilities
  • Prepare dishes according to recipes and restaurant standards.
  • Ensure food is prepared fresh, consistently, and to a high standard.
  • Maintain a clean, organized, and hygienic kitchen.
  • Monitor food quality, freshness, and proper storage.
  • Assist with food preparation and kitchen organization throughout the day.
  • Work efficiently with the kitchen team during busy service periods.
  • Follow all food safety and hygiene procedures.
Qualifications
  • Previous kitchen or cooking experience is an advantage.
  • Good understanding of basic cooking techniques.
  • Ability to follow recipes and established procedures.
  • Good organization and time management skills.
  • Ability to work calmly and efficiently under pressure.
  • Positive attitude and willingness to work as part of a team.
